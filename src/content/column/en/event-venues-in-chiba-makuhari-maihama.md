This series compares event venues in and around Tokyo area by area. This article covers Makuhari and Maihama in Chiba Prefecture. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](https://bizintokyo.com/en/) linked below.

Of the four venues here, three are hotels and one is Makuhari Messe (a large convention facility). At Makuhari Messe, the largest space, Makuhari Event Hall, has 3,948 fixed seats. The largest rooms at the hotels hold 1,400 to 2,500 people (theater style). Makuhari Messe is in Chiba City and the three hotel venues are in Maihama 1-chome, Urayasu City. Makuhari and Maihama are two different places.

## Where Makuhari and Maihama are

- They are outside Tokyo, in Chiba Prefecture. Makuhari Messe is in Mihama Ward, Chiba City, and the other three hotels are in Maihama, Urayasu City.
- **Makuhari**: From Tokyo Station, take the JR Keiyo Line to Kaihin-Makuhari Station. Makuhari Messe is about 5 min walk from the station.
- **Maihama**: From Tokyo Station, take the JR Keiyo Line to Maihama Station. To reach the hotels, take the free shuttle bus from Maihama Station South Exit, or take the Disney Resort Line to Bayside Station.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**Makuhari (Makuhari Messe)**

- From Narita Airport: there are direct buses to Makuhari Messe Chuo or Kaihin-Makuhari Station (according to Makuhari Messe's official information).
- From Haneda Airport: as from Narita, there are direct buses to Makuhari Messe Chuo or Kaihin-Makuhari Station (according to Makuhari Messe's official information).

**Maihama (three hotels)**

- From Narita Airport: the airport limousine bus (Airport Transport Service) "Tokyo Disney Resort Area – Narita Airport" route has stops at Hilton Tokyo Bay, Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel and Grand Nikko Tokyo Bay Maihama.
- From Haneda Airport: the airport limousine bus "Tokyo Disney Resort Area – Haneda Airport" route has stops at the same three hotels.
- Hilton Tokyo Bay's official website says the airport limousine bus is "operating with some restrictions." Check the limousine bus official website for the current service.

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Tokyo Station and change to the JR Keiyo Line (Kaihin-Makuhari Station for Makuhari, Maihama Station for Maihama).

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

## Where your guests can stay

- Hotels that are also venues: Grand Nikko Tokyo Bay Maihama, Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel and Hilton Tokyo Bay. Guests can stay in the same building as the event.
- Hotels connected to Makuhari Messe by a skyway (elevated walkway): Hotel New Otani Makuhari and APA Hotel & Resort Tokyo Bay Makuhari (according to the BizinTokyo nearby hotels page; their official websites say it is a 1 min walk).
- For hotels around each venue, compare the "10 best hotels nearby" pages on BizinTokyo, sorted by distance.
