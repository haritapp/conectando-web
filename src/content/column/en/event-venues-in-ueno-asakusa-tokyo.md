This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Ueno and Asakusa. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](https://bizintokyo.com/en/) linked below.

The five venues in this article are one hotel, two rental halls, one facility with exhibition rooms and one banquet hall (restaurant). The only hall with more than 1,000 seats is Asakusa Public Hall (1,070 seats). Asakusa View Hotel is directly connected to Asakusa Station on the Tsukuba Express, and airport limousine buses run directly to it from both Narita and Haneda.

## Where Ueno and Asakusa are

- Ueno Station is on the JR Yamanote Line. From Tokyo Station, you can reach it on the Yamanote Line without changing trains.
- You can reach Asakusa from Ueno Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- The Keisei Skyliner runs to Keisei Ueno Station without a transfer. Ueno Seiyoken is about 5 min walk from the Keisei Ueno Station Main Exit.
- Airport limousine buses run directly to Asakusa View Hotel.
- Trains for Narita Airport leave from Asakusa Station on the Toei Asakusa Line.

**From Haneda Airport**

- Airport limousine buses run directly to Asakusa View Hotel.
- Trains for Haneda Airport leave from Asakusa Station on the Toei Asakusa Line.

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Tokyo Station, then take the JR Yamanote Line to Ueno Station (one transfer). Ueno Station also has a shinkansen transfer gate.

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.
