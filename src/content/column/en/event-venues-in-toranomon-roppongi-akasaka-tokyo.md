This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Toranomon, Roppongi and Akasaka. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](#venue-guides) linked below.

This area has 19 main venues: 7 hotels, 11 rental halls and 1 small to mid-size space. The largest room is Heian at The Okura Tokyo, with 2,300 (theater). Three hotels have rooms for 2,000 people or more: The Okura Tokyo, Hotel New Otani Tokyo and ANA InterContinental Tokyo. Five of the hotels have direct buses from Narita Airport.

## Where Toranomon, Roppongi and Akasaka are

- The venues are in Minato City (Toranomon, Akasaka and Roppongi) and Chiyoda City (Nagatacho and Kioicho).
- All the nearest stations in the comparison table are subway stations.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- Airport limousine buses (Tokyo Airport Transportation) run directly to The Okura Tokyo, ANA InterContinental Tokyo, Grand Hyatt Tokyo, Hotel New Otani, Tokyo Garden Terrace Kioicho and The Capitol Hotel Tokyu.
- The JR Narita Express stops at Tokyo Station and Shinagawa Station. From there, change to the subway or another line.

**From Haneda Airport**

- On Tokyo Airport Transportation's official route page (Haneda Airport – Roppongi/Akasaka area), the only stop listed is Hotel New Otani.
- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho and change there.

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Shinagawa or Tokyo Station, then change to the subway or another line.

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

## Roppongi area in numbers

According to [Tokyo MICE Hubs](https://tokyomice.org/roppongi/index.html), an official site run by the Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau, the Roppongi area has:

- 13 convention facilities (about 24,800 sqm)
- 50 banquet halls
- 4,726 hotel guest rooms

These figures are for "Roppongi" as defined by that site. It does not say whether venues in Toranomon or Akasaka are included. It also does not say whether the figure of about 24,800 sqm is the total for all the convention facilities or the size of the largest one.

## Where your guests can stay

- Hotels that are also venues: The Okura Tokyo, Hotel New Otani Tokyo, ANA InterContinental Tokyo, Grand Hyatt Tokyo, The Ritz-Carlton Tokyo, The Capitol Hotel Tokyu and Andaz Tokyo. Guests can stay in the same building as the event.
- Hotels directly connected to a station: The Capitol Hotel Tokyu (Kokkai-gijidomae and Tameike-sanno stations), The Ritz-Carlton Tokyo (Roppongi Station) and Andaz Tokyo (Toranomon Hills Station).
