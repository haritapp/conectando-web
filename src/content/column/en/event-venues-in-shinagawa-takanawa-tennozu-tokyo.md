This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Shinagawa, Takanawa, Tamachi and Tennozu, including a venue at Haneda Airport. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see the venue guides on BizinTokyo linked below.

Of the 14 venues in this article, 2 are hotels and 7 are rental halls. The largest room is Konron at Grand Prince Shin Takanawa, with 2,880 people in theater style. TAKANAWA GATEWAY Convention Center also has a room for 1,924 people in theater style. On the Konan Exit side of Shinagawa Station are Shinagawa Intercity Hall, Shinagawa The Grand Hall, Shinagawa Season Terrace Conference and Tokyo Conference Center Shinagawa, all 2 to 6 minutes on foot from the station. The Prince Sakura Tower Tokyo and Grand Prince Shin Takanawa have direct buses from Narita Airport.

## Where Shinagawa, Takanawa, Tamachi and Tennozu are

- The venues are spread across Minato City (Konan, Takanawa, Shibaura, Mita), Shinagawa City (Higashi-Shinagawa, Kita-Shinagawa) and Ota City (Haneda Airport).
- From Tokyo Station, you can reach Shinagawa Station on the JR Yamanote Line without changing trains. Tamachi, Takanawa Gateway and Osaki are also stations on the Yamanote Line.
- The venue at Haneda Airport is Bellesalle Haneda Airport, in Terminal 3.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- The JR Narita Express runs to Shinagawa Station without a transfer.
- Airport limousine buses run directly to The Prince Sakura Tower Tokyo, Grand Prince Shin Takanawa and Takanawa Gateway Station (JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo).

**From Haneda Airport**

- Take the Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Takanawa Gateway, Tamachi or Osaki (one transfer).
- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Tennozu Isle Station (for the Tennozu venues).
- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Tamachi (one transfer).
- Bellesalle Haneda Airport is about 4 minutes on foot from Haneda Airport Terminal 3 Station.

**From the shinkansen**

- Shinagawa is a station on the Tokaido Shinkansen.
- Or get off at Tokyo Station, then take the JR Yamanote Line to Shinagawa (one transfer).

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

## Takanawa & Shinagawa area in numbers

According to the "Takanawa & Shinagawa" page of [Tokyo MICE Hubs](https://tokyomice.org/shinagawa/index.html), an official site run by the Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau, the Takanawa and Shinagawa area has:

- 8 convention facilities (the largest is 2,397 sqm)
- 124 banquet halls
- 5,484 hotel guest rooms

These figures are for "Takanawa & Shinagawa" as defined by that site. It does not say whether the venues in Tamachi, Tennozu, Osaki and Haneda Airport are included.
