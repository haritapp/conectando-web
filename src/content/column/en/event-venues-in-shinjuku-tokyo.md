This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Shinjuku. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see the venue guides on BizinTokyo linked below.

Of the 14 venues in this article, 9 are rental halls, 3 are hotels, 1 is a unique venue (Shinjuku Sumitomo Building Sankaku Hiroba) and 1 is a small to mid-size space. Nine venues have a largest room for 1,000 people or more. In Nishi-Shinjuku, five venues are within 4 minutes on foot of Tochomae Station: Bellesalle Nishi-Shinjuku, Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo, Hilton Tokyo, Shinjuku Sumitomo Hall and Shinjuku Sumitomo Building Sankaku Hiroba. Airport limousine buses from both Narita and Haneda stop at Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo, Hilton Tokyo and Hotel Sunroute Plaza Shinjuku.

## Where Shinjuku is

- The venues are spread around Shinjuku Station, Nishi-Shinjuku, Higashi-Shinjuku and Shin-Okubo, Takadanobaba and Waseda, and Hatsudai. Most are in Shinjuku City; Bellesalle Shinjuku Minamiguchi and Hotel Sunroute Plaza Shinjuku are in Shibuya City.
- From Tokyo Station, you can reach Shinjuku Station on the JR Yamanote Line without changing trains.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- The JR Narita Express runs to Shinjuku Station without a transfer.
- Airport limousine buses stop at Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo, Hilton Tokyo, Hotel Sunroute Plaza Shinjuku, Busta Shinjuku and Shinjuku Station West Exit (in front of Keio Department Store).

**From Haneda Airport**

- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Shinjuku (one transfer).
- Take the Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Shinjuku (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses stop at Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo, Hilton Tokyo, Hotel Sunroute Plaza Shinjuku, Busta Shinjuku and Shinjuku Station West Exit (in front of Keio Department Store).

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Shinagawa or Tokyo Station, then take the JR Yamanote Line to Shinjuku (one transfer).

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

## Where your guests can stay

- Hotels that are also venues: Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo, Hilton Tokyo and Hotel Sunroute Plaza Shinjuku. Guests can stay in the same building as the event.
- Hotel directly connected to a station: Hilton Tokyo (the hotel's official website says it is connected to Nishi-Shinjuku Station and Tochomae Station by an underground passage).
