This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Shibuya and Ebisu. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](https://bizintokyo.com/en/) linked below.

Shibuya is home to many IT companies, startups and creative firms. For tech and startup events, it is one of the first areas people consider, along with Roppongi. Both hotels and rental halls here have large rooms for more than 1,000 people. The area around Shibuya Station is in the middle of a large redevelopment, so station exits and passageways sometimes change. In Ebisu, The Westin Tokyo has its event rooms and guest rooms in the same building, and there is a direct airport bus from Narita.

## Where Shibuya and Ebisu are

- Shibuya is on the west side of central Tokyo. The center of the area is Shibuya Station, and Ebisu Station is the next stop on the JR Yamanote Line.
- From Tokyo Station, you can reach Shibuya on the JR Yamanote Line without changing trains.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- The JR Narita Express runs to Shibuya Station without a transfer.
- Airport limousine buses run directly to Shibuya Station, Cerulean Tower Tokyu Hotel and The Westin Tokyo.

**From Haneda Airport**

- Take the Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Shibuya (one transfer).
- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Shibuya (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses run directly to Shibuya Station, Cerulean Tower Tokyu Hotel and Shibuya Excel Hotel Tokyu. There is no direct bus from Haneda to The Westin Tokyo.

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Shinagawa or Tokyo Station, then take the JR Yamanote Line to Shibuya (one transfer).

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

## Shibuya area in numbers

According to [Tokyo MICE Hubs](https://tokyomice.org/shibuya/index.html), an official site run by the Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau, the Shibuya area has:

- 15 convention facilities (the largest is 2,541 sqm)
- 19 banquet halls
- 3,549 hotel guest rooms

These figures are for "Shibuya" as defined by that site. It does not say whether The Westin Tokyo in Ebisu (Meguro City) is included.

## Where your guests can stay

- Hotels that are also venues: Cerulean Tower Tokyu Hotel and The Westin Tokyo. Guests can stay in the same building as the event.
- Hotel directly connected to Shibuya Station: Shibuya Excel Hotel Tokyu (inside Shibuya Mark City).
- For hotels around each venue, compare the "10 best hotels nearby" pages on BizinTokyo, sorted by distance.
