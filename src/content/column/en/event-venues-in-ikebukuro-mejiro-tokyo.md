This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Ikebukuro and Mejiro. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](https://bizintokyo.com/en/) linked below.

The eight venues compared in this article are 5 rental halls, 1 hotel, 1 unique venue (a special place such as a historic building) and 1 small to mid-size space. The largest room is the theater at Tokyo Tatemono Brillia HALL, with 1,248 seats. Only two venues have a room for more than 1,000 people: Brillia HALL and Gakushuin Centennial Hall. OWLSPOT THEATRE is directly connected to an exit of Higashi-Ikebukuro Station on the Tokyo Metro Yurakucho Line. At Hotel Metropolitan Ikebukuro, the event rooms and the hotel are in the same building, and airport limousine buses come from both Narita and Haneda.

## Where Ikebukuro and Mejiro are

- The area is in Toshima City, Tokyo. All eight venues in this article are in Toshima City.
- The center is Ikebukuro Station, a stop on the JR Yamanote Line. Mejiro Station is also on the JR Yamanote Line, one stop from Ikebukuro.
- From Tokyo Station, you can reach Ikebukuro Station on the JR Yamanote Line without changing trains.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- Take the Keisei Skyliner to Nippori, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Ikebukuro (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses run without a transfer to Sunshine City Prince Hotel, Ikebukuro Sunshine Bus Terminal and Hotel Metropolitan Ikebukuro.

**From Haneda Airport**

- Take the Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Ikebukuro (one transfer).
- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, then change to the JR Yamanote Line for Ikebukuro (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses run without a transfer to Ikebukuro Station West Exit, Hotel Metropolitan Ikebukuro, Sunshine City Prince Hotel and Ikebukuro Sunshine Bus Terminal.

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Tokyo Station, then take the JR Yamanote Line to Ikebukuro (one transfer).

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

| [OWLSPOT THEATRE](https://bizintokyo.com/en/venues/owlspot/) | Rental hall | Theater: 301 seats (fixed seating) | Directly connected to Higashi-Ikebukuro Station Exits 6 and 7 (Yurakucho Line) / 10 min walk from JR Ikebukuro Station East Exit |

Capacities are the figures published on each venue's official website. The official sources are listed in each venue guide. Theater style (chairs only) holds the most people; layouts with desks or food hold fewer.

OWLSPOT THEATRE will close for renovation from April 1 to the end of December 2027 (planned). New bookings for the theater are not being accepted.

Jiyu Gakuen Myonichikan is an Important Cultural Property, so uses that do not suit the building may be turned down.
