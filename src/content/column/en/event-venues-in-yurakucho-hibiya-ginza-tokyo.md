This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Yurakucho, Hibiya and Ginza. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](https://bizintokyo.com/en/) linked below.

The largest room in this area is Hall A at Tokyo International Forum, with 5,012 seats (fixed seating). Three venues have rooms for more than 1,000 people: Tokyo International Forum, Imperial Hotel Tokyo and Yomiuri Hall. Tokyo Midtown Hibiya and The Peninsula Tokyo are connected to Hibiya Station. Two of the venues are hotels: Imperial Hotel Tokyo and The Peninsula Tokyo.

## Where Yurakucho, Hibiya and Ginza are

- The venues are in Chiyoda City (Yurakucho, Hibiya, Uchisaiwaicho and nearby) and Chuo City (Ginza and Kyobashi).
- Yurakucho Station is one stop from Tokyo Station on the JR Yamanote Line and Keihin-Tohoku Line. Tokyo International Forum is about 5 min walk from Tokyo Station.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- Airport limousine buses (Tokyo Airport Transportation) stop at Imperial Hotel Tokyo.

**From Haneda Airport**

- On Tokyo Airport Transportation's official bus stop page for the Imperial Hotel, the only route listed is the one to and from Narita Airport.

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Tokyo Station. Tokyo International Forum is about 5 min walk away.

For directions to the other venues, and for journey times and fares, which change by time of day, check each operator's official website and each venue guide.

Capacities are the figures published on each venue's official website. The official sources are listed in each venue guide. Theater style (chairs only) holds the most people; layouts with desks or food hold fewer. In halls with fixed seating, the number of seats is set.
