Planning a conference, seminar or other event in Tokyo for up to 300 people? Here are 16 venues that fit, listed with capacity, venue type, area and nearest station, based on each venue's official website (as of October 2026).

Contact venues early. Some start taking bookings a year in advance, and there is no downside to asking early. When you ask, they should be able to tell you when they start taking bookings.

## How capacity is counted here

The numbers in this article are for each venue's largest room, with chairs only, set in rows facing the front (theater style). Add tables or serve food, and the number goes down. At Hotel Sunroute Plaza Shinjuku, for example, the Fuyo room holds 250 with chairs only, but 150 for a standing reception.

Before choosing a venue, it is also worth asking whether that many people will really come. Even if your company runs large events at home, your first event in Japan may not need the same headcount. Conectando has seen many events that drew a small crowd the first time and never ran again (→ [How to organize an event in Tokyo](https://www.conec-tando.com/en/column/how-to-organize-an-event-in-tokyo/)).

## Up to 100 people

### CIC Tokyo

https://bizintokyo.com/en/venues/cic-tokyo/

- Largest room: Venture Café
- Capacity: approx. 60–80 seated plus approx. 40 standing (layout not specified)
- Nearest station: directly connected to Toranomon Hills Station (Hibiya Line)
- Note: Venture Café is not available at all on Thursdays. Events must end by 9 p.m. (per the official venue guide)
- *The official web page and the official PDF guide give different capacity figures (to be confirmed)

https://bizintokyo.com/en/venues/royal-park-hotel-iconic-shiodome/

- Largest room: Shiosai A+B (25th floor)

### SHIBUYA QWS Scramble Hall

https://bizintokyo.com/en/venues/shibuyaqws/

- Largest room: Scramble Hall
- Chairs only: approx. 200 / Party: approx. 100
- Nearest station: directly connected to Shibuya Station

### Global Business Hub Tokyo

https://bizintokyo.com/en/venues/global-business-hub-tokyo/

- Largest room: Event space
- Capacity: up to approx. 200 (layout not specified) / Classroom style: approx. 100
