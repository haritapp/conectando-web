This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Odaiba and Ariake. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](https://bizintokyo.com/en/) linked below.

There are seven venues in this area: two hotels, one arena, one large convention facility and three rental halls and similar spaces. TOKYO ARIAKE ARENA has up to about 15,000 seats, and the largest room at the Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba hotel holds 3,300 people theater style. Both hotels (Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba and Hilton Tokyo Odaiba) are directly connected to Daiba Station on the Yurikamome Line.

## Where Odaiba and Ariake are

- This is the Rinkai Fukutoshin (Tokyo waterfront sub-center) area on Tokyo Bay. It covers Odaiba (Minato City) and Ariake (Koto City).
- From Tokyo Station, take the JR line to Shimbashi Station and change to the Yurikamome Line. The Yurikamome runs from Shimbashi to Toyosu and stops at Daiba, Tokyo Big Sight and Ariake-tennis-no-mori Stations.
- From Shinjuku and Shibuya Stations, the Rinkai Line runs to Kokusai-tenjijo Station without a transfer.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- Take the JR Narita Express to Tokyo Station. From there, change to the Yurikamome Line at Shimbashi Station, as described in "Where Odaiba and Ariake are" above.
- Airport limousine buses serve stops at Hilton Tokyo Odaiba, Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba and Tokyo Bay Ariake Washington Hotel. Tokyo Big Sight and Ariake Garden are not stops on this route.

**From Haneda Airport**

- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Tennozu Isle Station and change to the Rinkai Line. Get off at Kokusai-tenjijo Station (for Tokyo Big Sight, TFT Hall and Ariake Central Tower) or Tokyo Teleport Station (for Hilton Tokyo Odaiba and Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba) (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses run directly to stops at Hilton Tokyo Odaiba, Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba, Tokyo Big Sight and Ariake Garden (the building that houses Bellesalle Ariake Conference Center).

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Tokyo Station, take the JR line to Shimbashi Station and change to the Yurikamome Line.
- If you get off at Shinagawa Station, the Rinkai Line's official website says to change at Shinagawa Seaside Station.

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

| [Bellesalle Ariake Conference Center](https://bizintokyo.com/en/venues/bellesalle-ariake-conference/) | Meeting and training facility | Room 5+6+7 (combined): 228 (classroom, with desks) | About 4 min walk from Ariake-tennis-no-mori Station (Yurikamome) |

Capacities are the figures published on each venue's official website. The official sources are listed in each venue guide. Theater style (chairs only) holds the most people; layouts with desks or food hold fewer.

For Hilton Tokyo Odaiba's Pegasus (full room), a PDF on the official website gives 1,600 for theater style, which differs from 1,564 (to be confirmed). We have not chosen one figure, so please check with the venue.

For using the whole of Ariake Central Tower, the official website gives the capacity in two different ways: "500–1,500 people" and "300–1,800 people" (to be confirmed).

The sample layout (PDF) on TFT Hall's official website shows 986 seats with a stage.

The International Conference Hall in Tokyo Big Sight's Conference Tower has 1,000 seats (992 fixed seats plus 8 movable seats). The official website does not give chairs-only capacities for the exhibition halls. The figure of about 15,000 seats for TOKYO ARIAKE ARENA is the maximum, including the 2,928 movable seats. The BizinTokyo venue guide does not give capacities by layout.

TFT Hall is operated by Tokyo Big Sight, but it is a separate building from the exhibition halls. Tokyo Big Sight Station (Yurikamome) and Kokusai-tenjijo Station (Rinkai Line) are also two different stations.

## Odaiba and Ariake area in numbers

According to the "Waterfront" page of [Tokyo MICE Hubs](https://tokyomice.org/waterfront/index.html), an official site run by the Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau, the area has:

- 12 convention facilities
- 33 banquet halls
- 4,380 hotel guest rooms

These figures are for "Waterfront" as defined by that site. The page names Tokyo Big Sight, Hilton Tokyo Odaiba and Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba, but it does not say whether all seven venues in this article are included.

## Where your guests can stay

- Hotels that are also venues: Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba and Hilton Tokyo Odaiba. Guests can stay in the same building as the event.
- Hotels directly connected to a station: both of the hotels above are directly connected to Daiba Station on the Yurikamome Line.
- For hotels around each venue, compare the "10 best hotels nearby" pages on BizinTokyo, sorted by distance.
