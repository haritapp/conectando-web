This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Kanda, Akihabara, Iidabashi and Suidobashi. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](#venue-guides) linked below.

Of the 18 venues covered in this area, 15 are rental halls, 2 are hotels and 1 is Tokyo Dome City (a mixed-use complex). Seven of the rental halls are Bellesalle venues, in Akihabara, Iidabashi Ekimae, Iidabashi First, Jimbocho, Jimbocho Annex, Kanda and Kudan. The largest room among the rental halls is the full 2F HALL at Bellesalle Akihabara (865 in theater style). The large banquet halls at the hotels are Grand Hall Tsubaki at Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo, for 2,000 people, and Tenku at Tokyo Dome Hotel, for 1,266 people (both in theater style). Three venues are within 3 minutes on foot of JR Akihabara Station (Akihabara Convention Hall, Akihabara UDX and Bellesalle Akihabara). Tokyo Dome Hotel is inside Tokyo Dome City.

## Where Kanda, Akihabara, Iidabashi and Suidobashi are

- The area is inside Tokyo's 23 wards. The venues are split between the Kanda, Akihabara, Jimbocho and Iidabashi side of Chiyoda City and the Suidobashi, Koraku and Sekiguchi side of Bunkyo City.
- From Tokyo Station, you can reach Akihabara Station on the JR Yamanote Line or Keihin-Tohoku Line (no transfer).
- Suidobashi Station is a stop on the JR Chuo-Sobu Line (local trains).

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- The JR Narita Express stops at Tokyo Station. It does not stop at Kanda, Akihabara, Suidobashi or Iidabashi, so change at Tokyo Station to the JR Yamanote Line or Keihin-Tohoku Line for Akihabara (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses run directly to Tokyo Dome Hotel and Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo.

**From Haneda Airport**

- Take the Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, then change to the JR Yamanote Line or Keihin-Tohoku Line for Akihabara (one transfer).
- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, then change to the JR Yamanote Line or Keihin-Tohoku Line for Akihabara (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses run directly to Akihabara Station, Tokyo Dome Hotel and Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo.

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Tokyo Station, then take the JR Yamanote Line or Keihin-Tohoku Line to Akihabara (one transfer).

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.
