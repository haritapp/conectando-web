This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Tokyo Station, Marunouchi, Otemachi and Nihonbashi. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see the venue guides on BizinTokyo linked below.

This area has 20 main venues: 13 rental halls, 5 hotels and 2 small to mid-size spaces. The largest room is Aoi at Palace Hotel Tokyo (1,440, theater). Many venues are directly connected to Otemachi Station. Seven of them are connected to the station directly or by an underground passage: Global Business Hub Tokyo, Tekko Executive Lounge & Conference Rooms, Four Seasons Hotel Tokyo at Otemachi, Nikkei Hall, Otemachi Mitsui Hall, Otemachi Sankei Plaza and Palace Hotel Tokyo.

## Where Tokyo Station, Marunouchi, Otemachi and Nihonbashi are

- This article covers venues in Marunouchi and Otemachi (Chiyoda City), and in Yaesu, Nihonbashi, Kyobashi and Nihonbashi Kakigaracho (Chuo City).
- The center of the area is JR Tokyo Station. Some venues are about 1 min walk from Tokyo Station (Shangri-La Tokyo, Marunouchi Building Hall and JP Tower).
- Venues a little farther from Tokyo Station are near Otemachi, Mitsukoshimae, Nihombashi, Kyobashi and Suitengumae subway stations.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- The JR Narita Express runs to Tokyo Station without a transfer.
- Airport limousine buses run directly to Tokyo City Air Terminal (T-CAT) and Tokyo Station Yaesu North Exit [Tekko Building]. To find out whether there are direct buses to the Marunouchi side or to the hotels, check the official website of Tokyo Airport Transportation (Airport Limousine).

**From Haneda Airport**

- Take the Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, then change to a JR line for Tokyo Station (one transfer).
- Take the Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, then change to the JR Yamanote Line or Keihin-Tohoku Line for Tokyo Station (one transfer).
- Airport limousine buses run directly to Tokyo Station Yaesu North Exit [Tekko Building] and Tokyo City Air Terminal (T-CAT).

**From the shinkansen**

- Tokyo Station is served by the Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen, and by the Tohoku, Yamagata, Akita, Hokkaido, Joetsu and Hokuriku Shinkansen. Get off at Tokyo Station and you are already in the center of the area.

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

## Marunouchi and Nihonbashi area in numbers

Tokyo MICE Hubs, an official site run by the Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau, has two pages that cover this area.

**[Marunouchi](https://tokyomice.org/marunouchi/index.html) (Marunouchi, Otemachi and Yurakucho)**

- 25 convention facilities
- 72 banquet halls
- 2,873 hotel guest rooms

**[Nihonbashi](https://tokyomice.org/nihonbashi/index.html)**

- 6 convention facilities
- 9 banquet halls
- 443 hotel guest rooms

These figures follow the areas as defined by that site. The Marunouchi page includes Yurakucho. We could not confirm which page the venues in Yaesu, Kyobashi and Nihonbashi Kakigaracho belong to, so not every venue in this article may be included.

## Where your guests can stay

- Hotels that are also venues: Palace Hotel Tokyo, Royal Park Hotel, Four Seasons Hotel Tokyo at Otemachi, Mandarin Oriental, Tokyo and Shangri-La Tokyo. Guests can stay in the same building as the event.
- Hotels directly connected to a station: Royal Park Hotel (Suitengumae Station), Four Seasons Hotel Tokyo at Otemachi (Otemachi Station) and Palace Hotel Tokyo (connected to Otemachi Station by an underground passage).
- For hotels around each venue, compare the "10 best hotels nearby" pages on BizinTokyo, sorted by distance.
