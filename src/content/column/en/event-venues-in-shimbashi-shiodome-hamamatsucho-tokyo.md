This series compares event venues in Tokyo area by area. This article covers Shimbashi, Shiodome, Hamamatsucho and Shibakoen. For step-by-step directions to each venue, see [the venue guides on BizinTokyo](#venue-guides) linked below.

This area has 11 venues: 5 hotels, 4 rental halls, 1 rental meeting room facility and 1 facility with exhibition halls. The largest room is the Ballroom at Prince Park Tower Tokyo, with 3,600 (theater, chairs only). Besides this hotel, Tokyo Prince Hotel and Bellesalle Shiodome also have rooms for more than 1,000 people. Four venues are about 1–4 min walk from Shiodome Station. Direct limousine buses from Narita Airport serve Conrad Tokyo, Tokyo Prince Hotel, Prince Park Tower Tokyo, Hotel InterContinental Tokyo Bay and Royal Park Hotel Iconic Shiodome.

## Where Shimbashi, Shiodome, Hamamatsucho and Shibakoen are

- The area is in Minato City, Tokyo. Of the 11 venues, only Bellesalle Shiodome is in Ginza, Chuo City.
- From Tokyo Station, you can reach Shimbashi Station and Hamamatsucho Station on the JR Yamanote Line without changing trains.

## Getting there from the airports and the shinkansen

**From Narita Airport**

- Airport limousine buses (Tokyo Airport Transportation) run directly to the 5 places below. This is stated on each hotel's official website.
  - Conrad Tokyo (bus stop on the 1st floor)
  - Tokyo Prince Hotel
  - Prince Park Tower Tokyo
  - Hotel InterContinental Tokyo Bay (Takeshiba)
  - Royal Park Hotel Iconic Shiodome
- Take the JR Narita Express to Tokyo Station, then change to a JR line for Shimbashi Station (one transfer).

**From Haneda Airport**

- The Tokyo Monorail runs to Hamamatsucho Station without a transfer. From Hamamatsucho Station to Shimbashi Station, change to a JR line.
- You can also take the Keikyu Line and Toei Asakusa Line to Shimbashi Station (as shown on the official website of Royal Park Hotel Iconic Shiodome).
- For limousine buses to and from Haneda Airport, the official websites of Conrad Tokyo, Tokyo Prince Hotel and Prince Park Tower Tokyo say the service is suspended (checked on October 6, 2026).

**From the shinkansen**

- Get off at Tokyo Station, then take the JR Yamanote Line to Shimbashi Station (one transfer).

Journey times and fares change by time of day, so check each operator's official website.

Capacities are the figures published on each venue's official website. The official sources are listed in each venue guide. Theater style (chairs only) holds the most people; layouts with desks or food hold fewer. Classroom style means rows of desks with chairs.

For Sanbo Hamamatsucho, the official materials do not say how the chairs are set for the figure of 880.

For the B1F HALL at Bellesalle Shiodome, the figure of 1,260 is from the venue page on the official website. The facility list page on the same website gives a maximum of 1,036 seats in theater style.

For directions to Bellesalle Shibakoen, the venue page on the official website says "directly connected to Shibakoen Station Exit A1b", while the facility list page says "1 min walk from Shibakoen Station Exit A1". The website also says the new entrance on the site with a direct connection is "scheduled for completion in August 2026".

Bellesalle Shiodome and Bellesalle Tokyo Shiodome Conference Center are two different venues in different locations. Tokyo Prince Hotel and Prince Park Tower Tokyo are also two different hotels.

## Hamamatsucho, Takeshiba and Shibaura area in numbers

According to the "SHIBA, Tokyo Bay" page of [Tokyo MICE Hubs](https://tokyomice.org/takeshiba/index.html), an official site run by the Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau, Hamamatsucho, Takeshiba and Shibaura have:

- 20 convention facilities
- 111 banquet halls
- 2,704 hotel guest rooms

These figures are for Hamamatsucho, Takeshiba and Shibaura as defined by that site. It does not say whether Shimbashi, Shiodome or Shibakoen are included.

## Where your guests can stay

- Hotels that are also venues: Prince Park Tower Tokyo, Tokyo Prince Hotel, Conrad Tokyo, Royal Park Hotel Iconic Shiodome and Hotel InterContinental Tokyo Bay. Guests can stay in the same building as the event.
- Hotel directly connected to a station: Hotel InterContinental Tokyo Bay (directly connected to Takeshiba Station on the Yurikamome).
- For hotels around each venue, compare the "10 best hotels nearby" pages on BizinTokyo, sorted by distance.
